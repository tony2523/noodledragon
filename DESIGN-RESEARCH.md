## Design decision

**Serious craft. Playful spirit.**

Position Noodle Dragon as an independent New Zealand design and development studio making macOS apps, mobile apps and mobile games. The dragon remains the signature; the surrounding design becomes quieter and more precise.

- Warm cream, deep green-black, cherry red, muted mint and lilac, with noodle-yellow accents.
- Large, restrained sans-serif headlines paired with expressive italic serif accents.
- Equal treatment of the three capabilities, with original CSS illustrations rather than fabricated product screenshots.
- Clear navigation: What we make → Our approach → The studio → Contact.
- A direct email contact rather than a form with no working backend.
- A responsive menu that closes after selection, outside clicks or Escape; visible keyboard focus; reduced-motion support.
- Preserve the existing logo and mascot assets. Do not invent client logos, reviews, metrics, awards, product availability or app-store links.

## Future product plan

Per Tony’s instruction, do not show the unnamed macOS Markdown viewer or Moustache Monsters yet. When actual releases are ready, insert a “Made by Noodle Dragon” section after the capabilities. Use real screenshots, a short benefit-led description, supported platforms and a verified product/download link. Keep the current capability section as the studio-level overview.

The existing logo artwork includes the historic “Game Design Studio” subtitle. It has been preserved rather than altered; a future brand-artwork refresh could broaden that subtitle to “Apps & Games”.
